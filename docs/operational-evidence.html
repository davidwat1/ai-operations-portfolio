Publication gates and privacy

Gate A — Configuration: enabled schedule and last-run timestamp. Gate B — Execution: verified run completion and exceptions. Gate C — Operational quality: sampled coverage and reconciled dispositions. Gate D — Business impact: measured outcome improvement against a documented baseline.

Only Gate A is supported by the published scheduler snapshot. A private application tracker has been located, but its row-level contents, correspondence and live job-search status are intentionally excluded from this public page. The presence of that tracker does not independently validate run reliability or business impact.

Evidence standard

Design intent, observed configuration, verified run results, and validated business impact are different maturity levels. Performance claims will be published only with a defined period, denominator, supporting records, and exceptions. Private correspondence, credentials, and individual application records are not published.
