I use the evidence-based stories in this portfolio to practice concise executive answers that demonstrate ownership, judgment and measurable impact without reciting my résumé.

This is a local practice worksheet. Responses and scores are not sent to a server or saved between visits. Confidential client or personal information should not be entered.

1. Choose the leadership example

Competency: scaling through leaders

Tell me about a time you scaled an organization while protecting delivery performance.

Review STAR / CARL and 60-second narrative

2. Deliver and capture your answer

Give your answer aloud first. Target roughly 60–90 seconds. Lead with the scope and business challenge, explain your personal decision, land the result, then stop.

3. Score the delivery

Use a 1–5 scale: 1 = unclear or unsupported; 3 = adequate; 5 = crisp, specific and credible. This is your self-assessment, not an independently observed score.

15 / 25

Baseline self-rating. Score after speaking, then identify the single biggest improvement.

Copy produces a text summary you can paste into interview coaching. Scores are not historical trend data unless you deliberately retain the summaries.

4. Test the harder questions

  1. What decision did you personally make, rather than the team?
  2. What competing business priorities or trade-offs did you manage?
  3. Which evidence proves the result, and what remains an estimate?
  4. What did you change in the operating model so the improvement would persist?
  5. What would you do differently with what you know now?

For CARL, treat the Learning element as a reflection you can substantiate—not a claim that you recorded the insight at the time.
