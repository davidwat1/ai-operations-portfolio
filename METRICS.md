# Measurement and publication controls

| KPI | Numerator | Denominator | Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Automation coverage | Eligible tasks executed without manual work | All eligible tasks | Task-level audit log | Baseline pending |
| SLA attainment | On-time completed scheduled runs | Expected scheduled runs | Scheduler timestamps | Baseline pending |
| Decision quality | Audited decisions meeting criteria | Decisions audited | Review rubric and samples | Baseline pending |
| Lead disposition time | Time from ingest to verified disposition | Leads with both timestamps | Timestamped tracker | Baseline pending |
| Qualified advancement | Qualified leads advanced | Qualified leads reviewed | Reconciled application tracker | Baseline pending |

**Evidence tiers:** Concept / Design / Prototype / Operational (observed) / Validated outcome. Never collapse tiers. Keep synthetic records separate from production records. Record measurement windows and sample sizes with each published statistic.
