# Executive Evidence Audit — Portfolio Standard

Status: working framework. This document is a method, not a claim of verified outcomes.

## Purpose
Maintain one defensible evidence record per executive accomplishment, then derive consistent portfolio case studies, resume bullets, LinkedIn language, and 30/60-second interview answers.

## Required record
- **Employer / period:** Verify dates and title.
- **Business situation:** What problem or opportunity existed?
- **Decision owned:** What did the executive personally decide or authorize? Distinguish sole authority, shared authority, and recommendation.
- **Accountable scope:** P&L, budget, team size, geography, customers, and governance remit, each with source/qualification.
- **Leadership intervention:** One or two consequential actions and their rationale.
- **Business outcome:** Before/after metrics, timeframe, customer validation, or carefully qualified qualitative outcome.
- **Trade-offs and resistance:** Alternatives considered, stakeholder alignment, risks, and why the decision was appropriate.
- **Evidence status:** Verified / user-reported / needs validation / not publishable.
- **Disclosure:** Public-safe summary; redact client-sensitive, employee, and proprietary information.
- **Activity-only warning:** Flag descriptions of participation, meetings, or oversight that omit a decision and outcome.
- **Panel questions:** Three substantive questions the available evidence does not yet answer.

## Outputs and review gates
1. **Public case study:** Business context → decision → accountable scope → intervention → outcome. Publish only approved, non-confidential details.
2. **Resume:** One concise bullet centered on ownership and outcome, with no unsupported attribution.
3. **LinkedIn:** Searchable executive positioning consistent with the resume.
4. **Interview library:** 30-second and 60-second versions; practice questions and defensible follow-ups.

Never invent numbers, results, titles, decision rights, or causal attribution. Mark gaps explicitly and ask for supporting details. Avoid private compensation, client contracts, personnel details, and confidential operational metrics in the public repository.

## Initial audit queue
Experis; Microsoft/Xbox Certification; Atos; Kymeta; CompuCom. Each requires a separate evidence record and publication review.

## Quality checklist
- Does the first sentence show a business decision, not merely activity?
- Is the scale attributable and accurately qualified?
- Is the outcome independently grounded or explicitly described as self-reported?
- Can the executive defend trade-offs and authority under panel questioning?
- Are public, resume, LinkedIn, and interview versions consistent?
